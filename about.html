About

Product Strategist · Essayist · Peregrinator

A Life Between Systems and Territories

I was born in Alsace, left at 24, and have since lived between Sofia, Varna, and Yerevan. I speak the languages of software architecture and of ancient roads. I am a father, a son, an observer of the lines that connect people to places, ideas to infrastructures.

My professional life is built around the design of digital systems — APIs, product strategies, software architectures — and the conviction that durable structures require both technical precision and human understanding.

What I Bring to a Team

  • Technical Product Strategy — from business logic to resilient architectures.
  • API & SDK Design — scalable, developer-friendly, open-first.
  • Fractional Product Leadership — I work with teams that need direction without a full-time hire.
  • Systems Thinking — I see software not as code, but as a living structure that reflects the organizations that build it.

Why I Write

I write to understand. My essays explore the intersections of geopolitics, technology, and the human condition of displacement. I draw from history, architecture, and classical literature — not as ornament, but as method.

Every system is a story. Every story is a system.

Selected Experience

2015–2020 — Technical Product Management (e-learning platforms, APIs, developer tools)
2009–2015 — Software Engineering & Systems Design (Eastern Europe & Caucasus)
